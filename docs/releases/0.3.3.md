## What's new

- **Automatic updates on macOS.** From 0.3.3 on, Kitty on the Mac updates itself, like on Windows. If you have an older version, download the DMG below once.
- **Linux packages.** `.AppImage`, `.deb` and `.rpm` for x64, plus a one-line installer for the terminal.
- **Interface languages.** English (the new default) and Portuguese (Brazil), in Settings > General and on the welcome screen.
- **Back/forward swipe respects sideways scrolling.** On spreadsheets, maps and other pages that scroll sideways, the two-finger swipe scrolls the page and only goes back or forward at the edge.
